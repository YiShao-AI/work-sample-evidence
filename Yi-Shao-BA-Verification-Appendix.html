ATM prospecting workflow

Before
Search maps one location at a time, copy listings into Excel, and rely on memory to avoid missed areas or duplicate review.
→
Scope a market, forecast usage, search adaptively, qualify and dedupe candidates, record decisions, and advance a governed pipeline.

Owner
Action
System behavior

Owner / BD
Sets the market, search shape, categories, and budget.

BD operator
Reviews candidate evidence and exceptions.

BD owner
Records qualification, reason, stage, owner, and next action.
Persists history, revisit dates, and campaign membership.

Owner / analyst
Exports current records and reviews market/campaign performance.
Reconciles candidate counts, workflow cost, and closed-campaign outcomes.
Coverage and outcome metrics remain tied to their scope and period.

Deployment control checklist
Access: named roles, least privilege, periodic membership review, credential rotation, and device expectations.
Content: approved source classes, local-first processing, minimized message output, and a defined escalation path.
Lifecycle: raw, derived, index, log, cache, and backup records follow named retention and deletion owners.
Operations: monitoring, cost/error thresholds, backup/recovery, incident response, change record, and rollback path.
Messaging channel: Telegram was the company-standard communication platform, so the support assistant met agents inside their existing workflow. Channel access, retention, minimized output, and incident handling belong in the same operating controls as the rest of the system.
