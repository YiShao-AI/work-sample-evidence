Project 2 · Prospecting operations

Turn manual map research into a scalable prospecting process

The application turns one-at-a-time map research into a repeatable prospecting process. Existing sites, pipeline records, and new candidates share one operating view; each search is scoped, metered, cached, and connected to a qualification decision.

Open live demo View repository
